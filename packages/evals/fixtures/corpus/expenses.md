# Expense policy

## What you can claim

Travel, client meals and home-office equipment up to 400 EUR per year are reimbursed.

## Receipts

Upload every receipt in the expenses app within 30 days of the purchase. Claims without a receipt are rejected.

## Per diem

When travelling for work you receive a per diem of 65 EUR per day for food. Hotels are booked through the travel desk, not claimed.
