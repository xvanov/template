# Incident response

## Severity levels

SEV1 means customers cannot use the product. SEV2 means a major feature is broken. SEV3 is everything else.

## Paging

For a SEV1 the on-call engineer is paged immediately and must acknowledge within 15 minutes. SEV2 pages during business hours only.

## Postmortems

Every SEV1 and SEV2 gets a blameless postmortem, written within 5 business days and shared with the whole company.
