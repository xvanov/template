# Onboarding

Welcome to Northwind. This page covers your first week.

## Before day one

IT ships your laptop to your home address. It arrives pre-enrolled in device management; do not wipe it.

## Accounts

You get an email account, a chat account and a password manager vault on day one. Your manager adds you to the team channels.

## First week

Pair with your onboarding buddy every day. By Friday you should have shipped one small change to production.
