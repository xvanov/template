# Customer support SLA

## Response times

P1 tickets (service down) get a first response within 1 hour, around the clock. P2 tickets get a response within 4 business hours. P3 tickets within 2 business days.

## Business hours

Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 CET, Monday to Friday.

## Escalation

If a P1 is not resolved within 4 hours, the support lead escalates it to the engineering on-call.
